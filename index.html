House Guidelines

Rules & Policies

We keep it simple — respect the space, look out for each other, and everyone has a great time.

  • Choose an available check-in time when booking.
  • Check-out is automatic, exactly 22 hours after check-in.
  • Same-day check-in requires at least 3 hours' notice.
  • A 2-hour turnover gap after checkout is included in availability.
  • Do not exceed maximum occupancy per unit.
  • Children under 12 must be supervised at all times.
  • Outside visitors not allowed without prior notice.
  • Excess guests charged per head (see rate cards).
  • No illegal drugs, firearms, or dangerous items.
  • No outside food or drinks (corkage applies).
  • No loud noise after 10:00 PM.
  • No pets inside the resort premises.
  • Guests are liable for any damage to property.
  • 7+ days before check-in: Full refund.
  • 3–6 days before: 50% refund of down payment.
  • Under 3 days: No refund. Rescheduling once allowed.
  • Force majeure: Full refund or free rescheduling.
  • Guests with open wounds may not use the pool.
  • Shower before entering. No lotion in pool area.
  • Children must wear life vests in deep pools.
  • No glass containers allowed near the pool.
