Payment & Cancellation Rules

  • Payment is via GCash only. Slot confirmed once payment is verified.
  • Down payments are non-refundable but may be used to reschedule once.
  • Cancellation 7+ days before visit: full refund.
  • Cancellation 3–6 days before: 50% refund of down payment.
  • Cancellation under 3 days: no refund, one free reschedule allowed.
  • Bad weather or force majeure: full refund or free rescheduling.
