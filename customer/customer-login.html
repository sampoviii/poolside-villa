Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Poolside Villa. By creating an account and using our online reservation system, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

  1. Account Registration & Accuracy: You must provide true, accurate, and complete personal information (full name, valid email address, and active mobile number) when registering an account.
  2. Reservation & Down Payment Policy: All reservations require a 50% package down payment (plus full LPG fee if availed) via GCash, accompanied by a valid proof of payment receipt for verification.
  3. Check-In, Stay Duration & Clean-Up Buffer: Standard resort reservations cover a 22-hour stay starting from your chosen check-in time, followed by a mandatory 2-hour sanitation and preparation buffer.
  4. Cancellation & Rescheduling: Down payments are non-refundable once verified, except in cases of force majeure or resort-initiated closures. Rescheduling requests are subject to date availability and management approval.
  5. Resort Rules & Property Care: Guests are expected to observe resort safety rules, maximum guest capacity per package, and proper care of villa facilities. Any damage to property may be charged accordingly.

Data Privacy Agreement (RA 10173)

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, Poolside Villa is committed to protecting and respecting your personal information.

  • Information We Collect: We collect your first name, last name, email address, phone number, reservation details, and uploaded GCash payment receipts strictly for account management and booking verification.
  • Purpose of Processing: Your personal data is used solely to process resort reservations, verify payments, send booking or password-reset notifications, and maintain customer support records.
  • Data Protection & Security: Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing, and access to personal and payment records is restricted to authorized Poolside Villa administrators and caretakers.
  • Non-Disclosure to Third Parties: Poolside Villa will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with unauthorized third parties unless required by law.
  • Your Rights as a Data Subject: Under RA 10173, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data by contacting Poolside Villa management.
